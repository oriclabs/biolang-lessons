of those content grants:

- Dengue virus 1 complete genome, `NC_001477.1`.
- *Mycobacterium leprae* TN complete genome, `NC_002677.1`.

The records are pinned by accession version, byte size, and SHA-256 checksum in
`lesson.json` so the exercises remain reproducible.
