[*A Little Book of R for Bioinformatics*](https://a-little-book-of-r-for-bioinformatics.readthedocs.io/en/latest/).
The source book is licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

The chapter order, example questions, and exercises are adapted with attribution.
OriClabs translated the R/SeqinR operations to BioLang, updated file handling for
browser lessons, added executable BioLang calculations, and edited explanations
for the Studio format. All four figures displayed
by the source chapter are included byte-for-byte in `figures/source`, including
the original unseeded histogram. No replacement random dataset is supplied.
This is not an official edition and is not
endorsed by the original author.

The adapted instructional collection and copied source figures are distributed
under CC BY 3.0. Repository-authored tooling explicitly marked MIT remains MIT.
The two FASTA records are exact NCBI RefSeq data files and are excluded from both
of those content grants:

- Dengue virus 1 complete genome, `NC_001477.1`.
- Rabies virus complete genome, `NC_001542.1`.

Every file is pinned by byte size and SHA-256 checksum in `lesson.json` so the
exercises remain reproducible.
