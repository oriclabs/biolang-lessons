# Source map

Upstream: Stephen D. Turner, *Biological Data Science with R*, Chapter 12,
revision `f16caf3d3df4ac27297b87f50c37c7c024471208`.

| BioLang coverage | Upstream coverage | Fidelity evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sections 12.1-12.3 and Exercises 1-8 | Background, import, and poor man's DGE | Exact 38,694-gene airway count matrix and eight-sample metadata; totals, positive-gene counts, fold changes, and both scatterplots are recomputed by BioLang. |
| Section 12.4 and Exercises 9-12 | DESeq2 analysis | The browser does not imitate DESeq2. `validation/reference.R` independently runs R 4.5.2 with historical DESeq2 1.44.0; its checksum-pinned result table reproduces the rendered chapter counts and is inspected in BioLang. |
| Section 12.5.1 | CRISPLD2 count plots | Exact DESeq2-normalised count oracle; both analytical plots are regenerated by BioLang with the source grouping, log scale, fills, and labels. |
| Section 12.5.2 and Exercises 13-14 | MA and volcano plots | Exact historical result rows and significance classes; BioLang regenerates both plots. |
| Sections 12.5.3-12.5.5 | VST, PCA, and heatmap | PCA coordinates, variance percentages, top-25 VST matrix, gene/sample order, annotations, and clustering are pinned from the historical oracle; BioLang renders the PCA and clustered heatmap. |
| Sections 12.6-12.7 | Reproducibility and pathway-analysis preparation | Provenance and pathway-analysis inputs are retained. No proprietary pathway-analysis result is invented. |

## Image boundary

The source's `img/countdatacoldata.png` is a generic explanatory schematic, not
an output of the airway analysis. The IPA pathway/network images in section
12.7 are externally generated proprietary report illustrations. They are not
copied and are not presented as BioLang output. All source analytical plots—raw
and log mean-count scatterplots, both CRISPLD2 count views, MA plot, volcano
plot, PCA, and clustered heatmap—are generated by BioLang.
