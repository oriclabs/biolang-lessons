# Attribution

This lesson adapts the opening fishing case study in Chapter 13 of Matthew
Russell's *Statistics in Natural Resources: Applications with R*. The online
book is licensed under
[CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/), and the
adapted instructional material remains under that licence.

`fishing.rda` is copied byte for byte from the official `stats4nr` repository at
commit `69da7a618857d63ffbe56ba5d3b56e5b836ddbb9`. The package declares CC0.
`fishing.csv` is a mechanical `write.csv` export of that exact object.
`fishing-histogram.png` is the exact PNG served by the published chapter.

No synthetic data or independently drawn replacement figure is included. The
scope ends before regression because BioLang's current Poisson implementation
does not reproduce the published model. BioLang syntax and fidelity checks were
adapted by OriClabs on 2026-09-03.
